    Weapons & rules

      Aim down sights with right-click, a two-finger tap on a trackpad, or Z (the Aim button on phones). A quick tap toggles aim on, holding aims only while held. Fire with click or F, from the hip or while aimed. On phones you can drag the Fire button to steer while shooting: aiming tightens spread and zooms in, and is the only way to use the sniper. You always carry a pistol and can hold one picked-up weapon: picking up another drops yours. Ammo runs out, so grab ammo crates. Health regenerates after 5 seconds without taking damage. You respawn 3 seconds after dying.

      Phone controls

      Rotate your phone to landscape for the best experience.

      Connections are peer-to-peer (WebRTC). The host’s browser runs the match. On the same wifi, traffic stays on your local network. Rooms also work across the internet on most networks.
